Hikam Seminary

A comprehensive 2-year modular program in traditional Islamic sciences tailored specifically for adult women balancing work, family, and spiritual growth.

Curriculum Structure

Structured Study Rooted in Tradition

The Hikam Seminary offers a systematic progression through foundational disciplines including Classical Arabic Grammar, Quranic Tafsir, Hadith Studies, Fiqh (Jurisprudence), Aqeedah (Theology), and Purification of the Soul (Tazkiyah).

Designed for busy schedules, classes are conducted in interactive weekly sessions with direct access to experienced female scholars and instructors.
