Afsha Mohammed is a native Houstonian who joined Sabeel Institute in 2015 as a student and studied Islamic topics in depth for three years including Tafsir of the Quran, Arabic grammar, hadith, female companions of Prophet Muhammad SAW, and more. She eventually became a teacher and team member. She continued her journey to further her studies in Islamic subjects and is enrolled as an Alimiyyah student at Qalam Institute. Her favorite subject to teach is Seerah, which allows her to continually revisit Prophet Muhammad SAW’s life and helps her instill the love of his teachings.
