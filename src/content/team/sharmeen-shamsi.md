Sharmeen Shamsi teaches Intermediate Arabic at Sabeel Institute. She graduated from the ‘Alimiyyah program at the Institute of Islamic Education in 2007. She has a bachelor’s degree in education from Benedictine University. She has taught Arabic, Seerah, Tafsir, and Fiqh in various Islamic institutes for women over the years. She recently obtained ijazah in the ten variant recitations of the Qur’an.
