Afsha Mohammad is a native Houstonian who joined Sabeel Institute in 2015 as a student and completed its three-year Classical Islamic Knowledge certification, studying Tafsir of the Quran, Arabic grammar, hadith, female companions of Prophet Muhammad SAW, and more. She eventually became a teacher and team member. She continued her Islamic studies at Qalam Institute and has since graduated with an ‘Alimiyyah degree. Her favorite subject to teach is Seerah, which allows her to continually revisit Prophet Muhammad SAW’s life and helps her instill the love of his teachings.
