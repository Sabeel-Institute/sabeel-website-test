Sobia Ansari completed her Bachelors in Science in 2011 and pursued her love of teaching in private and public schools. She began her studies with the Classical Islamic Knowledge program at Sabeel Institute in 2016. After the completion of the three year program, she continued to take other courses with Sabeel Institute. She teaches youth classes to instill the love of our deen in the younger generation.

Sobia is also a member of the marketing team and manages email communications for Sabeel Institute’s upcoming courses.
