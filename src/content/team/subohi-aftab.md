Subohi Aftab began Islamic studies at Sameera Institute in 2015 and later graduated with an Alimiyyah degree. A mother of three, she has over a decade of volunteer experience in youth programs and at weekend Islamic schools. Subohi is currently a full-time teacher at Sabeel Institute.
