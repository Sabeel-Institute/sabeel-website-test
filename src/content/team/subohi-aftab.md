Subohi Aftab began her Islamic studies at Sabeel Institute in 2015 and later completed her ‘Alimiyyah degree. She has since taught Arabic, Fiqh, and Imam al-Haddad’s Book of Assistance in Sabeel Institute’s Hikam Foundations program. A mother of three, Subohi also brings over a decade of volunteer experience in youth programs and weekend Islamic schools.
