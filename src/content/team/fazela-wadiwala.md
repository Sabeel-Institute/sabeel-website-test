Fazela Wadiwala was born and raised in the United Kingdom. She has a Bachelor’s Degree in Nursing. She moved to the U.S. in 2009. She began her pursuit of Classical Islamic knowledge at Sabeel Institute in 2015 and completed four years of studies in courses including Quran, Hadith, Fiqh and Arabic. She then continued her pursuit at Sabeel Institute taking part time courses in a variety of subjects. Fazela has been teaching courses part time at Sameera Institute & especially enjoys teaching children and the youth with a focus at Bear Creek Islamic Center.
