Anchored Hearts is a monthly gathering for sisters to learn, reflect, and connect. Through beneficial Islamic talks, meaningful conversations, and occasional social activities, each gathering offers a welcoming space to nurture faith and build sisterhood.

> Come as you are, leave with a heart a little more anchored.

## This month: Holding Onto Faith in a Changing World

**Friday, October 16, with Dr. Sabeen Najam**

Discover how to keep your faith strong and your heart grounded in a world where faith is increasingly challenged.

The halaqa is open to all sisters! We will start with an optional creative activity with Sr. Hira Jeddy from House of Makers. Get creative and enjoy an evening of jewelry-making, snacks, and sisterhood!

- **Optional activity includes:** make your own pearl bracelet and earrings, snacks, and House of Makers favors
- **Activity registration deadline:** Wednesday, October 7. Special Sabeel one-time rate of $15 (regularly $25)
