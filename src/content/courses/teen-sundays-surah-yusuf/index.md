## The Quran hits different when you see yourself in it

Have you ever wondered where Allah’s plan is in everything you’re going through?

Surah Yusuf is more than the story of Prophet Yusuf (AS). It is a story about facing hardship, making difficult choices, staying true to who you are, and trusting Allah — even when you cannot see what comes next.

Together, we’ll explore questions that connect Surah Yusuf to our own lives:

- How can we stay strong when life feels uncertain?
- Who are we when no one is watching?
- Can waiting and patience bring us closer to Allah?
- How should we handle success?
- What can forgiveness teach us about strength?
- How can we trust Allah while our story is still unfolding?

Through meaningful discussions, reflection, and good company, students will explore resilience, patience, identity, forgiveness, and trust in Allah’s plan.
