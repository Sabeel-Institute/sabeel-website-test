Our journey toward Allah is one of continuous growth, reflection, and striving. How do we nurture our hearts, strengthen our relationship with Allah, and embody the qualities of a believer in our everyday lives?

Join us for Journey to Excellence, an eight-part series exploring essential spiritual qualities rooted in the Qur’an and Sunnah. Through reflection and practical guidance, we will discover how to cultivate these qualities, deepen our connection with Allah, and strive toward excellence in our worship, character, and daily lives.

Together, we’ll reflect on how these qualities help us grow in faith, character, and ultimately acquire ihsān — excellence in our relationship with Allah and in the way we live our lives.

A journey of small steps, meaningful growth, and becoming better for His sake.

## The eight sessions

### 1. Tawbah: The Beauty of Returning to Allah

**October 6, with Sr. Fazela.** Returning to Allah is an essential part of our spiritual journey, no matter where we find ourselves. Explore the true meaning of Tawbah and discover how sincere repentance opens the doors to Allah’s forgiveness, mercy, and renewed closeness. Reflect on the transformative blessings of continually turning our hearts back to Him.

### 2. Dhikr: Reviving the Heart Through Remembrance

**October 13, with Sr. Israa.** In the busyness of everyday life, how do we keep our hearts connected to Allah? Explore the transformative power of Dhikr and its essential role in nurturing our spiritual well-being. Discover how consistent remembrance can revive our hearts, bring tranquility, deepen sincerity in worship, and keep us focused on our ultimate purpose.

### 3. Zuhd: Cultivating Detachment from the Dunya

**October 20, with Sr. Afshan H.** What does it truly mean to practice Zuhd? Is it about giving up worldly comforts, or cultivating a deeper relationship with Allah? Explore the true meaning of Zuhd and discover how to develop a heart that engages with the world without becoming attached to it, keeping the pleasure of Allah at the center of our lives.

### 4. Shukr: Living a Life of Gratitude

**October 27, with Sr. Sana M.** Inspired by the Prophet’s ﷺ words, “Should I not be a grateful servant?”, explore the true meaning of Shukr and its essential place in the life of a believer. Discover the different dimensions of gratitude and reflect on practical ways to recognize Allah’s countless blessings and express gratitude through our hearts, words, and actions.

### 5. Sabr: The Strength of Steadfastness

**November 3, with Sr. Marwah.** Sabr is more than patience. It encompasses perseverance, restraint, and remaining steadfast in our relationship with Allah through every stage of life. Explore the different dimensions of Sabr, its significance in the Qur’an and Sunnah, and the immense rewards promised to those who embody it. Discover practical ways to cultivate steadfastness in everyday life.

### 6. Tawakkul: Finding Peace in Reliance on Allah

**November 10, with Sr. Anum.** What if true peace comes not from having everything under control, but from knowing Who is in control? Explore the meaning of Tawakkul and discover how knowing Allah strengthens our ability to trust Him through life’s uncertainties. Learn to balance sincere effort with complete reliance on Allah, Al-Wakil, while finding peace in entrusting our outcomes to Him.

### 7. Mujahadah: Striving Toward Allah

**November 17, with Sr. Hira.** What does it mean to sincerely strive for the sake of Allah? Explore the concept of Mujahadah and discover how our struggles and consistent efforts can become a means of drawing closer to Him. Reflect on the importance of sincerity, perseverance, and focusing on our efforts rather than outcomes as we strive to grow in worship and character.

### 8. Taqwa: Living with Consciousness of Allah

**November 24, with Sr. Jessica.** How can we cultivate a constant awareness of Allah in every aspect of our lives? Explore the deeper meaning of Taqwa as a spiritual shield that guides our decisions, shapes our character, and strengthens our relationship with Allah. Discover practical ways to navigate everyday challenges with God-consciousness, respond to mistakes with good deeds, and seek Allah’s guidance and ease.
