Hikam Foundations was designed to provide a foundational knowledge base for those interested in Islamic studies, covering the essential topics needed to practice Islam confidently, inshaAllah.

The program was offered free of charge, to empower participants with the knowledge and confidence to live their faith fully and contribute meaningfully to their communities. It included a special offering, **“Why Islam Is True?”**, a course on evidence-based insights into the fundamental truths of Islam.
