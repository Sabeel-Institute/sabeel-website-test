Ever wanted to understand the Arabic of the Quran or recite with proper tajweed without feeling overwhelmed? Quran Immersion is a 10-week, one-day-a-week course designed to build your foundational skills in classical knowledge and prepare you for Hikam Foundations.

## 1. Arabic Language: Guided Introduction

Unlock the foundations of the Arabic language in a clear, systematic, and supportive environment. Using the acclaimed primer *10 Lessons of Arabic* (Das Sabaq), this course breaks down core grammatical structures into bite-sized concepts, bridging the gap between beginner-level study and functional comprehension.

## 2. Quran Tafsir

Reconnect your heart to the majesty of the Quran through an in-depth thematic study of Surah Qaf. Revealed in the Makkan period, this powerful surah addresses the core realities of existence — the resurrection, divine power, the recording of human deeds, and the proximity of Allah to His creation.

## 3. Tajweed: Applied Recitation of Surah Qaf

Learn to recite the Book of Allah with clarity, accuracy, and confidence. This course pairs beginner-friendly rules of tajweed directly with the recitation of Surah Qaf, so you practice what you learn on a specific section of the Quran.
