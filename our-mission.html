Empowering Women in Sacred Learning

Sabeel Institute was established in Houston with a distinct vision: to provide women with a sacred space for rigorous, traditional Islamic study led by qualified female scholars and educators.

We strive to bridge authentic classical knowledge with contemporary context, enabling women to serve as spiritual pillars in their homes, communities, and professions.
