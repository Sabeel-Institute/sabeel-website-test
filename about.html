WHO WE ARE

A women-led home for Islamic learning in Houston.

Since 2009, Sabeel has brought women together for structured, in-person Islamic learning in Houston. Today, its programs serve adult women, university students, teens, and children.

MISSION

Sound knowledge for faithful lives.

Sabeel equips women and families through structured, in-person study rooted in the Qur'an, Sunnah, and scholarly tradition—nurturing sound knowledge, faithful practice, and service.

OUR STORY

Built by women, growing across generations.

Since 2009, Sabeel has grown from one teacher offering classes to university students and adult women in masjids across southwest Houston into a teaching faculty of seven Alimaat.

Graduates of Sabeel's own programs continue their studies and now serve in teaching, administration, and program support. What began with adult classes now includes learning for children and teens—forming an intergenerational community rooted in knowledge, suhbah, and service.
