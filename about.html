Academic Rigor & Spiritual Growth

We are a non-profit Islamic educational institution, dedicated to providing authentic Islamic education to our community through qualified teachers. The philosophy of our courses is distinct in its embrace of the entire academic tradition of the Ahl al-Sunnah wal-Jama’a with respect for all scholars of Tafsir, Hadith, Fiqh, and Usul al-fiqh.

Our mission is to help our students develop strong Islamic identities through love for Allah (SWT), His Prophet (SWS), and good company (sohbah), while providing them a thorough understanding of their religion that is both intellectually and spiritually uplifting.
